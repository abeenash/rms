ZyloServe Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

ZyloServe is a restaurant management app for restaurant staff, operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us"). Staff use it to take table and counter orders, update them, and record payments for the restaurant they work for. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Who can use the app

ZyloServe is only for staff of restaurants that use the Zylo system. Accounts are created by the restaurant's administrator, not inside the app. The app is not intended for children.

Information we handle

  • Staff account details: your name, email address, phone number, role and the restaurant you work for. These come from the account your restaurant created for you.
  • Sign-in details: your email and password are sent securely to our servers to sign you in. After you sign in, a session token and your basic profile are stored in your device's secure storage so you stay signed in.
  • Order information: the orders you create or change, including menu items, quantities, prices, table numbers, discounts, payment status and payment method, and the name and phone number recorded on the order.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, the app shows alerts on your device about your own activity, such as a successful sign-in. These alerts are created on your device.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. It does not contain advertising or third-party analytics.

How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To let you and your restaurant create, update and settle orders.
  • To produce bills, invoices and kitchen tickets when you choose to print or share them.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Your restaurant: its owners and administrators can see orders and staff activity in the Zylo system.
  • Service providers: we use hosting and infrastructure providers to run our servers. They process data only on our behalf.
  • When you share: if you share a bill or invoice, it goes to the app or person you choose.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information when required by law.

Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Session tokens are kept in the device's secure storage and removed when you sign out.

How long we keep information

We keep account and order information for as long as your restaurant uses ZyloServe, and as long as needed to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Your choices and deleting your data

To access, correct or delete your account or personal information, ask your restaurant's administrator, or contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL]. Some order records may need to be kept for the restaurant's accounting and legal obligations.
